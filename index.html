About

Working at the animal–human–environment interface

[EDIT] Replace with headshot
headshot.jpg

Mohammod Misbah Uddin is a veterinarian and PhD Candidate in Veterinary Medical Sciences (Epidemiology) at the University of Calgary, expected to graduate in Spring 2027. He works across the Orsel Research Group and the Kutz Research Group, under the supervision of Dr. Karin Orsel.

His dissertation strengthens community-based wildlife health surveillance in the Canadian Arctic — evaluating surveillance capacity and estimating reductions in community exposure to Brucella under practical risk-reduction scenarios. The work spans surveillance evaluation, diagnostics and case definition for Brucella suis biovar 4 in caribou and muskoxen, and scenario-based exposure modeling, carried out in partnership with Kugluktuk, Cambridge Bay, and Ulukhaktok.

He combines quantitative modeling and surveillance evaluation with diagnostic coordination, data QA/QC, and standardized protocol development for public health and wildlife surveillance. He completed his MSc in Epidemiology and Public Health and his DVM at Sylhet Agricultural University in Bangladesh, and is a registered veterinarian with the Bangladesh Veterinary Council.
