About

Working at the animal–human–environment interface

[EDIT] Replace with headshot
headshot.jpg

Misbah is a DVM, MSc, and PhD Candidate in Veterinary Epidemiology at the University of Calgary, working in the Orsel Lab under the supervision of Dr. Karin Orsel. His doctoral research examines community-based wildlife health surveillance (CBWHS) across the Canadian Arctic, evaluating how well these systems detect and respond to disease in free-ranging caribou and muskox populations.

Within this work, he investigates the epidemiology of Brucella suis biovar 4 (BS4), building probabilistic exposure models to better understand the risk of transmission to people who harvest and handle wildlife. The research is carried out in partnership with the communities of Kugluktuk, Cambridge Bay, and Ulukhaktok, and sits at the One Health interface of animal, human, and environmental health.

His broader interests extend to zoonotic disease more generally, drawing on Bayesian statistics, spatial analysis, and mixed-methods research. He completed his DVM and MSc at Sylhet Agricultural University in Bangladesh.
